\section{Summaries, acknowledgements and declarations}
Edit \texttt{Abstract.tex} to replace both summaries. It uses an
\texttt{otherlanguage} environment for Dutch and returns to English afterwards.
Both summaries are unnumbered chapters with explicit table-of-contents entries.
The short chapter summaries use \texttt{absbox}; they serve a different purpose.

\begin{lstlisting}
\chapter*{Acknowledgements}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Acknowledgements}
Write your acknowledgements here.
\end{lstlisting}
The title and table-of-contents text should agree. This pattern is also used for
the back-matter lists. Remove the optional dedication as a whole page block,
including its page break, to avoid leaving an accidental blank page.

Annex 4 describes the author's declaration for the draft submitted to the
manuscript committee, including contributions and AI use~\cite{ut2026}.
Prepare it for your actual work; the example content here is not a declaration
of research authorship. Keep submission attachments distinct from the final
book unless instructed otherwise.

\section{Why these section filenames are familiar}
This chapter retains four section files whose contents now explain the template:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{Section\_Radar.tex}
\item \texttt{Section\_DetEst.tex}
\item \texttt{Section\_Deep\_Learning.tex}
\item \texttt{Section\_Metrics.tex}
\end{itemize}
The names preserve the original layout of the source tree; filenames do not
determine the printed headings. You can rename a section file later by updating
its corresponding \verb|\input| in \texttt{Chapter\_02.tex}.
