\section{The separate propositions insert}
The file \texttt{propositions.tex} shares the thesis metadata and uses its own
148 by 210\,mm trim area with 3\,mm bleed. It has no chapter tabs or cover image.
Its statements are examples about writing and methods; replace them with your
own defensible propositions.

Article 16 permits an optional loose insert of at most ten propositions:
at least four thesis-related and at most two general, with the rest academic.
Promotor approval is required~\cite{ut2026}. The example contains eight items
grouped accordingly, but their illustrative content is not an approved set.

\begin{lstlisting}
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=5pt]
  \item A proposition supported by the thesis. (This thesis)
  \item Another proposition, expressed as a defensible claim.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lstlisting}
Do not copy an entire \texttt{document} environment into a chapter. The insert
is a separate document, so open or compile it as its own entry point.
