\begin{absbox}
Replace the instructional content gradually, verify the complete document and prepare a clean source release. The structure can stay familiar while the writing and design become your own.
\end{absbox}

\section{A practical order of work}
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=5pt]
\item Edit \texttt{ThesisDetails.tex}: titles in both languages, author, date,
supervisors, identifiers and affiliation. Keep the real rector details required
for your defence rather than substituting an invented office holder.
\item Update committee rows and funding in \texttt{Title\_Preamb.tex}; replace
the summaries and acknowledgements. Remove the dedication if unwanted.
\item Replace chapter text and chapter abstracts while keeping the folders and
their figure locations. Add your actual references and publications to the
appropriate databases.
\item Declare your symbols and acronyms centrally. Remove unused example
declarations once their role is clear; do not remove the registry implementation.
\item Choose a palette, font and cover design, then check their effect on every
page style. Confirm the print geometry with your printer.
\item Replace or remove the illustrative propositions and obtain the required
approvals for the real thesis and its formal pages.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Checks after a complete build}
Read the log for undefined commands, missing files, unresolved citations,
duplicate destinations and rerun requests. Investigate overfull boxes rather
than assuming they are harmless. Check that figures and captions remain legible
at the final trim size, tables do not run into margins and long headings fit.

Follow a symbol link to the global list and its page link back to the first
occurrence. Confirm that an unused declaration stays out of the list, that
abbreviations expand as intended, and that your publication entries appear only
in Publications. Check the table of contents after the final pagination pass.
The small source checker in \texttt{tests} catches several structural mistakes,
but it cannot verify typography or actual PDF destinations.

\section{Remove example identity and data}
Search for Alex Morgan, Example Press, Example University, fictional publication
titles, \texttt{20XX}, the placeholder ISBN pattern and the template's chapter
titles. Replace the actual content fields; keep explanatory occurrences only
if you are distributing this guide itself. Do not copy the sample results table
or methodological claims into a real thesis as research findings.

The NASA photograph includes a credit file. Keep that file if you keep the
image; for other images, record the creator, original source and reuse terms.
The UT logo retains its institutional identity status. References and sample
publications are distinct even when both appear near the end of the book.

\section{Share a clean template repository}
Publish the contents of the template folder as the repository root. Keep
\texttt{Chapters}, each existing \texttt{fig} directory, \texttt{References} and
\texttt{tests} in their current locations. The repository README gives a short
entry point; the thesis chapters provide the detailed examples. A released
example PDF can be useful after a successful build, but it is a generated
deliverable, not the source of the layout.
