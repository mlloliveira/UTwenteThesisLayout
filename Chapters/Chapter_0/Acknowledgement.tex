\chapter*{Acknowledgements}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Acknowledgements}
An acknowledgement is a personal part of a thesis. Use this page to thank the
people whose advice, practical help and encouragement made your work possible.
Begin with the research community if that suits your voice, then move towards
friends and family. A sincere paragraph is often more effective than a long list.

For example: I thank my supervisors for helping me refine difficult questions,
my colleagues for generous discussions, and my family for their patience and
encouragement throughout this work.

Replace this example with your own words. Put contractual funding statements in
the colophon or wherever your funder requires them; personal thanks do not replace
those statements. This unnumbered chapter uses the quieter front-matter style.
