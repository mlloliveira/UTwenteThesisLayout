\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=1pt]
\item Keeping figures with their chapters makes the source of a thesis easier
to navigate.~(This~thesis)
\item Central symbol declarations can support consistent mathematical notation.~(This~thesis)

visible to the reader.~(This~thesis)
\item A small set of shared design controls simplifies visual revision.~(This~thesis)
\item Reproducible analysis requires documenting the assumptions as well as
the computational steps.
\item Reporting measurement uncertainty improves the interpretation of a
numerical result.
\item A clear question is often the most useful contribution to a discussion.
\item Time spent explaining a method can reveal where it needs improvement.
\end{enumerate}
